Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This application is a personal automation tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • It requests access to YouTube solely to upload videos and set their details (title, description, privacy, AI-content label).
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other user.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer (self-hosted n8n) and are never shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API usage is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
